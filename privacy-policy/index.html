1. Who we are

Betre Games is a sole proprietorship owned by Hazar Tunca and based in Türkiye. We are the controller of personal information processed through the Services. You can reach us at info@betregames.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide. Our Games do not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. If you email us, we receive your email address and anything else you include in your message.

Information collected automatically. When you play our Games, third-party services built into them may collect device and usage information, such as:

  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen settings and app version
  • Identifiers: advertising ID (such as the Google Advertising ID or Apple’s IDFA, where you allow it) and app-instance identifiers
  • Network information: IP address and the approximate location derived from it. We do not collect precise location
  • Usage information: in-game activity, session length, features used and ad interactions
  • Purchase information: what you bought, when, and a transaction identifier
  • Diagnostic information: crash reports and performance data

In-app purchases. Some Games offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads or buying in-game items. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never your payment card details. Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.

Information stored on your device. Game progress and settings are saved on your device. Uninstalling a Game deletes them.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Games, including fixing crashes and bugs
  • To process and deliver in-app purchases, restore purchases and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • To understand how the Games are used so we can improve them and develop new ones
  • To show ads, including personalized ads where you have allowed them, and to measure how well ads perform
  • To respond to your questions and requests
  • To protect the Services, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our rights
  • To meet legal obligations

4. Legal bases

Where Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) or the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performing our agreement with you so we can provide the Game; our legitimate interest in keeping the Services secure and improving them; our legal obligations; and your consent for personalized ads and, where required, analytics.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. We share information only in these cases:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us run the Services, such as analytics, crash reporting and advertising providers, listed below
  • Legal reasons. When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or Betre Games
  • Business changes. If Betre Games is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction

Our Games may use the services below. Each handles data under its own privacy policy. The services a specific Game uses, and the data they collect, are listed in that Game’s Data safety section on Google Play and its App Privacy section on the Apple App Store.

  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing
  • Apple App Store and Apple in-app purchases
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics)
  • Unity (Unity Ads, Unity Analytics)

Some advertising providers may treat sharing of identifiers for personalized advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” under certain US state privacy laws. See the next section for how to opt out.

6. Advertising and your choices

Some Games show ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can also:

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads. On iOS, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On iOS, we ask for your permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites, and the Games work if you decline
  • Turn off analytics or ad personalization where the Game offers a privacy settings screen

Opting out does not remove ads; it only makes them less relevant to you.

7. International data transfers

We are based in Türkiye, and our service providers may process information on servers outside Türkiye and outside your country, for example in the United States and the European Union. These transfers follow the safeguards required by applicable law, including Article 9 of KVKK and, where relevant, the GDPR.

8. How long we keep information

We keep emails for as long as we need them to handle your request and meet legal obligations. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies and retention periods.

9. Children

Our Games are meant for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or under the higher age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, including under KVKK, the EU and UK GDPR and US state privacy laws such as California’s, you may have the right to:

  • Know whether we process your personal information and get a copy of it
  • Correct inaccurate information or have it deleted
  • Learn who it has been shared with
  • Object to or restrict how it is used, and opt out of targeted advertising
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing
  • Not be treated unfairly for using these rights

To make a request, email info@betregames.com. We may need to verify your identity first, and we reply within 30 days. Because our Games do not use accounts, we may ask for your advertising ID or other details to find your data. You may also complain to your local data protection authority; in Türkiye, this is the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. This website

betregames.com does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. Continuing to use the Services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
