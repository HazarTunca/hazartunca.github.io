Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how Betre Games (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile games and apps (the “Apps”) and our website, betregames.com. Betre Games is a sole proprietorship owned by Hazar Tunca and based in Türkiye.

Information we collect

Information you give us. Our Apps don’t require an account, and we don’t ask for your name, email address or phone number. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Information collected automatically. Third-party services built into our Apps may collect device and usage information, such as device model, operating system version, language, advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, in-app activity and crash reports.

Information stored on your device. Game progress and settings are saved on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

How we use information

  • To run our Apps and keep them working, including fixing crashes and bugs
  • To understand how our Apps are used so we can improve them
  • To show ads, including personalized ads where you have allowed them
  • To reply when you contact us
  • To meet legal obligations

Third-party services

Our Apps may use the services below. Each one handles data under its own privacy policy. The services a specific App uses, and the data they collect, are listed in that App’s Data safety section on Google Play.

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics)
  • Unity (Unity Ads, Unity Analytics)

Advertising

Some Apps show ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can also reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. On Android, go to Settings > Google > Ads.

Sharing

We don’t sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, so they can provide their services to us, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep information

We keep emails for as long as we need them to handle your request. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies.

Children

Our Apps are meant for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698) and the EU and UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email info@betregames.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

This website

betregames.com doesn’t use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
