About

Betre Games is an independent mobile game studio founded in Türkiye. We make games designed to be played on a phone and publish them on Google Play and other mobile platforms.

  • Quick to pick up

    Our games open in seconds, and you know what to do within the first minute. They're made for short sessions on the bus, in a queue or on a break.

  • Smooth on any phone

    We optimize for the phones people actually use, not just the newest ones, and we keep battery life and storage in mind.

  • Better after launch

    A game isn't finished when it reaches the store. We keep updating and improving it based on what players tell us.
